Our Philosophy

Crafting Timeless Styles, Building Confidence

Our skilled barbers combine classic techniques with modern trends to deliver clean cuts, precision fades, and perfectly groomed beards. Every appointment is tailored to your style, ensuring exceptional service, premium care, and a relaxing atmosphere where you always leave looking sharp and feeling your absolute best.

From warm lather straight-razor shaves to bespoke scissor tailoring, Men Salon stands for unhurried craftsmanship and welcoming neighborhood tradition.

Our History

Three Decades of Craft

How a single neighborhood barber chair evolved into New York’s destination for refined men’s grooming.

1998

First Chair

It all started with a single chair, a pair of sharp scissors, and a passion for timeless barbering. From the very beginning, our focus was simple: great cuts, honest craft, and a welcoming experience.

2015

A New Generation

New barbers joined the journey, bringing fresh ideas and contemporary fade techniques while keeping the hot-towel traditions that defined Men Salon at the heart of every cut.

2026

Modern Flagship

Today our studio at 123 Barber Street welcomes over 10,000 returning gentlemen every year, uniting bespoke grooming consultations with contemporary architectural comfort.
